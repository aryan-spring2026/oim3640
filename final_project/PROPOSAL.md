## My Project Proposal

## What I'm building:
[For my final project, I want to build an application called MakeMyTrip. This is going to be an application that helps users get an understanding and comparison metric with respect to planning for international trips in a more smart way. A user will input critical factors such as their depature airport, destination(s), duration of trip, number of travelers, and month they desire to travel. The goal of this application is to help derive a total estimate of traveler's cost for their trip including factors such as flights, domestic transport, and expenses throughout the trip. Comparions between different trips will occur simulationsley.]

## Why I chose this:
[I am someone who enjoys to travel outside of the country, explore new opportunities, and step out of my comfort zone. When I plan my trips, it is hard to estimate how much I should budget and plan. Furthermore, picking destinations when having a monetary constraint is very difficult as well. I want to build an application that is for young people so they can travel internationally without financial constraints.]

## MVP:
## 1
User can input their desired departure airport, number of people joining them for the trip, duration of the trip, the months they intend to stay, and their respective final destination.

##2
Estimating how much flight tickets would cost, cost of housing, understanding how much it would cost to eat outside, how local transportation pricing works, and the total expenses the trip would cost.

##3
Ensure a user friendly and easy to understand application that users can spend minimal amount of time to help them understand how to maximize time with planning and budgeting international travel. 

##4
Showcasing easy to understand and concrete recommendations to users based on which trips are the most cost effective.
